  • The game works without an account and does not ask for your name, email, or location.

1Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Dungdungbot ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game TungTung: Save the Buddha (the "Game"), available on Google Play. By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

2Information we collect

Information you give us

The Game has no sign‑up or login, so we do not ask for personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. The only information tied to you is what you choose to send us if you contact support by email.

Game data stored on your device

The Game saves your play data locally on your device (for example: level progress and unlocks, coins and energy, hero health, and audio/settings preferences). This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Purchase information

If you buy an in‑app item (such as a coin pack), the purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we do not receive or store your credit‑card or payment‑method details. We receive a purchase confirmation (such as a purchase token/order ID) so we can deliver the item you bought.

Technical information

To download the Game and process purchases, Google Play and Google Play services may collect standard technical information (such as device type, operating‑system version, and a Google‑assigned identifier) as described in Google's own privacy policy. This is handled by Google, not by us.

This produces aggregated and de‑identified usage and diagnostic data. If you enable this section, name your provider here (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase) and link its policy.

  • To run the Game and save your progress on your device.
  • To deliver in‑app items you purchase and validate those purchases.
  • To respond to you when you contact support.

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7Data retention

Game data stored on your device remains until you delete it (by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling). Purchase records held by Google are retained by Google according to its policies and applicable law. Any support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and to keep records of the request.

8Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, and purchases run over Google's secure billing system. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Purchases: view or request refunds through your Google Play account.
  • Advertising ID if ads on: reset or limit it in your device's Google settings.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights over your personal information. Contact us and we will respond as required by law.

  • Internet / network access — to download content and process in‑app purchases.

  • Vibrate if used — for gameplay feedback.

The Game does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.
